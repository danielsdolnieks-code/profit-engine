The rules behind the numbers

Revenue
Price × units sold.

Contribution
Revenue − variable costs.

Company profit
Total contribution − all fixed costs, counted once.

Break-even sales
Fixed costs ÷ contribution ratio.

Margin of safety
Sales − break-even sales. A negative number means sales are below break-even.

Stopping a product
Sales and variable costs stop. Only the fixed costs you mark as avoidable disappear.

Overhead allocation
Splits shared costs between product reports. Changing this split does not change company profit.

Limited machine time
Compare contribution per machine hour. Priorities assume you can sell the additional units.
